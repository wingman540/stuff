### One-liner
**LinkedUp is a safety-first career network for teens (13–18):** explore real
careers, find first jobs and internships, and connect with *verified* mentors —
with guardian oversight and moderation built in.

### The problem
Young people make huge, expensive decisions about careers and education **blind**:
- They can't see what jobs are actually like, what they pay over a lifetime, or
  what training really costs.
- LinkedIn is built for adults and professionals; Handshake is gated to
  enrolled college students; TikTok career content is unverified and unsafe.
- The one place teens *could* get guidance — direct contact with professionals —
  is exactly where parents (rightly) won't let them go unsupervised.

### The insight / wedge
The reason no one serves this well is the reason it's defensible: **doing it
safely for minors is hard, expensive, and legally heavy.** We build the trust
layer — identity-verified mentors, background checks, guardian co-visibility,
real moderation — as the core product, not a bolt-on.

### What we've built
A working product (live demo: **https://wingman540.github.io/stuff/**):
- "Day in the Life" career videos · lifetime-earnings-vs-training-cost dashboard
- Verified mentors + monitored group sessions · safe messaging with safeguards
- Guardian dashboard (approvals + oversight) · jobs/internships from vetted
  employers · résumé builder
> *(Prototype today — front-end demo; the real safety backend is the build ahead,
> see ROADMAP.md.)*
